### [0:00 - 0:25] The Problem & Introduction
* **Screen:** Open ScamShield AI homepage showing the modern dark/neon cybersecurity dashboard.
* **Narration:**  
  *"Hello judges! In 2026, cyber fraud has become an epidemic targeting ordinary citizens through SMS, WhatsApp, and Telegram. ### [0:25 - 0:55] Case 1: Electricity Bill Disconnection Scam
* **Screen:** 
  1. Click the preset pill **"⚡ Electricity Cutoff Threat"**.
  2. The text *"Dear Consumer your electricity power will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM..."* populates the textarea.
  3. Click **"Scan for Threats"**.
  4. Watch the animated circular gauge swing to **88/100 (SCAM)**.
* **Narration:**  
  *"Let's test a common fraud: an urgent SMS claiming your power will be disconnected tonight. In less than 150 milliseconds, ScamShield AI delivers an 88/100 risk score. Notice how the interactive highlight engine isolates the red flags—artificial urgency coercing immediate panic, and a suspicious 10-digit mobile number pretending to be an official officer."*

### [0:55 - 1:25] Case 2: Fake Bank KYC APK & Link Forensics
* **Screen:**
  1. Click **"🏦 Fake Bank KYC APK"**.
  2. Click **"Scan for Threats"**.
  3. Gauge swings to **94/100 (SCAM)**.
  4. Scroll down to show **"Identified Threat Signals"** and **"Technical Link & Domain Forensics"**.
  5. Hover over the detected link table: point out `sbi-kyc-update.xyz`, dangerous TLD, typosquatting warning, and `.apk` detection.
* **Narration:**  
  *"Now let's examine a sophisticated banking phishing attack. Notice our Domain Forensics engine in action: it identifies that `sbi-kyc-update.xyz` is not the official SBI domain, warns of a dangerous throwaway `.xyz` TLD, and detects a direct malicious `.apk` file download designed to steal OTPs."*

### [1:25 - 1:50] Multilingual Support & Elder "Family Mode"
* **Screen:**
  1. Switch language dropdown in Navbar to **"हिंदी (Hindi)"** or **"मराठी (Marathi)"**. All verdicts, action steps, and summaries instantly translate.
  2. Click the **"Family Mode"** toggle in the Navbar, then click **"Elder / Family View"** button.
  3. Show the large-font, high-contrast modal with the warning: *"🛑 खतरा — यह फ्रॉड है! किसी भी लिंक पर क्लिक न करें..."*.
  4. Click **"Copy Warning Message for WhatsApp"** (checkmark appears).
* **Narration:**  
  *"Elderly family members are the most vulnerable to social engineering. ScamShield AI includes full multilingual support in Hindi and Marathi, alongside an innovative 'Family Mode.' In one click, complex technical jargon is translated into large-font, elder-friendly advice that can be shared straight to your family's WhatsApp group."*
