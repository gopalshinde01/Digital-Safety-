### Slide 2: The Crisis (Why Traditional Security Fails)
* **The Surge:** Over ₹7,000+ crore lost in India to mobile financial fraud in the past 12 months.
* **The Vectors:** Electricity bill cutoff panic, fake bank KYC updates, part-time job lures, and UPI refund traps.
* **The Root Problem:** Social engineering exploits human psychology, not software vulnerabilities. Traditional antivirus engines do not scan SMS urgency, fake APK links, or UPI PIN deception.
* **Vulnerable Demographics:** Elderly citizens and non-English speakers face the steepest barrier to understanding deceptive technical fraud.

---

### Slide 3: The Solution — 4-Layer Hybrid AI Architecture
* **Layer 1: Deterministic Heuristic Engine:** Instant regex pattern detection of urgent coercion, APK downloads, and UPI entrapment.
* **Layer 2: Technical Link Forensics:** Deep lexical URL analysis, typosquatted domain detection (SBI, HDFC, Paytm), and risky TLD flags.
* **Layer 3: Lightweight ML Classifier:** Scikit-learn TF-IDF + Calibrated Logistic Regression trained on thousands of augmented cybercrime samples (<5ms inference).
* **Layer 4: Generative LLM & Multilingual Layer:** Google Gemini 1.5 Flash for contextual reasoning, with bulletproof offline rule fallback in English, Hindi, and Marathi.
